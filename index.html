About Arte Fikrah

Arte Fikrah — meaning "Art of the Idea" — is a forward-thinking cultural festival founded in 2018. We bridge the rich artistic heritage of the Islamic world and Andalusian legacy with bold, contemporary creative practices.

From generative art installations to live electronic-maqam fusion performances, Arte Fikrah is where tradition collides with the avant-garde. Our mission is to create a global platform for artists who dare to reimagine cultural identity through a modern lens.

📍 Venue:Darunnajath Islamic Complex Vallapuzha
📅 Dates: 17 May 2026
🎟️ Entry: Free & open to all. Registration required for candidates.
